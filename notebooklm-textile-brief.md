# Textile Hero Brief

Create a single, full-bleed, natural textile image for a homepage hero.

Visual direction:
- 16:9 landscape composition
- Entire frame filled with woven teal fabric
- Very sharp macro thread detail
- Calm, premium, editorial mood
- Soft natural daylight
- Gentle folds across the surface
- One centered circular patch made from warm beige fabric

What this should feel like:
- tactile
- realistic
- quiet
- high-end
- material-focused

What to avoid:
- text
- labels
- charts
- icons
- infographic panels
- callouts
- human figures
- product staging
- decorative objects
- collage layouts

The result should resemble a refined fabric catalog photograph rather than an infographic.
